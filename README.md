# Paper Arcade

**Play:** https://mentalplatypus.github.io/PaperArcadeGames/

Small browser games drawn on graph paper. They run in any modern browser on desktop or phone, in light or dark mode, with nothing to install.

## Games

- **Snake**: eat apples, grow longer, avoid the walls and your own tail.
- **Tic-Tac-Toe**: play the computer or a friend on the same screen.
- **Minesweeper**: three board sizes, flags, and best times.
- **Ruler Merge**: slide equal lengths together to double them. Make a 128 cm line to win, working around the ink blots.
- **Sudoku**: three difficulty levels, pencil notes, and undo.
- **Dots & Boxes**: draw lines between dots and close boxes to claim them. Play the computer or a friend on 3×3, 4×4 or 5×5 boards.

Made by [David Wang](https://mentalplatypus.github.io/).
